    About this list

    What is included

    U.S. programs that offer a clinical elective, clerkship or comparable supervised rotation, accept international medical students, and let the student apply directly, whether or not the institution is also on VSLO. Observerships and placements sold through private agencies are not included.

    Where the data come from

    The starting list is the program-level dataset of a systematic environmental scan of 252 U.S. program records (LCME and osteopathic medical schools, the U.S. News Honor Roll hospitals and VSLO resources), searched in March and April 2024 and re-verified in June 2026.

    Zhang JMF, Heringer DL, de Oliveira Rodrigues G, Hernandez Oquet RE, Cardoso R, Liu R, Mathur M. Direct-Application Clinical Elective Pathways for International Medical Students in the United States: A Systematic Environmental Scan. Research Square; 2026. Preprint, not yet peer reviewed. doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-11613768/v1. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

    How it stays current

    Readers mark entries as correct or not correct and suggest new programs. Once a week the reports are compared with each program's official page. Clear changes are applied and listed under Recent updates. Unclear cases, new programs and removals wait for a decision by the maintainers, and the entry is marked as being re-checked in the meantime.

    Reader reviews

    Readers who rotated at a program can leave a short review. Reviews are anonymous personal experiences. Each one is read before it is published, but it is not verified with the program, and it does not change the requirements and costs shown, which come from the official page.

    Before you apply

    A requirement is shown as “Required” only when the official page documents it. “Not reported” means the page did not state the item, not that the requirement does not exist. Always confirm on the official program page. This is an independent list and is not affiliated with the institutions listed or with the AAMC.

    Your privacy

    Votes and reviews are anonymous. If you give an email with a report or suggestion it is used only to follow up on that message and is never published. If you ask for email alerts about an entry, your email is used only to send those alerts, it is never published or shared, and every alert tells you how to stop them.

    Visits are counted with Google Analytics only if you answer Yes to the question at the bottom of the page. Google Analytics sets cookies and records which programs, filters and searches are used here. Your email and what you write in the forms are never sent to it. You can change your answer at any time at the bottom of the page.
